Math format

Default delimiters for copied equations. The other format is always one right-click away, or on its own shortcut.

Keyboard shortcuts

Click a box, press the new combination, then save.

Shortcut rules
  • Use one or two of Ctrl, Alt, Shift (on macOS ⌘ and Ctrl too) plus a letter, digit, F-key, arrow, or Home/End/PageUp/PageDown/Insert/Delete/Space/Comma/Period.
  • Shift can't be the only modifier. F1–F12 can be used alone.
  • If Ctrl+Alt types characters on your keyboard layout (it acts as AltGr on Polish and some other layouts), pick another combination.
  • You can also change these in about:addons → ⚙ → Manage Extension Shortcuts.
